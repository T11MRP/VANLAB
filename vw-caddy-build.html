01 Carpet lining

Lined wall to wall

The whole load area is lined in charcoal carpet. Side walls, wheel arches, the sliding door and the rear door cards, all trimmed tight around the factory fixings.

The power panels are set flush into the lining so they look like they came with the van.

  • Charcoal automotive carpet throughout
  • Wheel arches wrapped in one piece
  • Sliding door and rear doors trimmed to match
02 Starlight roof

Stars from front to back

A fibre optic starlight headliner runs the full length of the van, from the cab right back to the rear doors. Hundreds of individual points in mixed colours, set into a black headliner.

LED spotlights are set into the roof for proper working light, on their own switch so the stars can stay on by themselves.

  • Fibre optic stars across cab and load area
  • Recessed LED spotlights on a separate switch
  • Embroidered Van Lab badge on the pillar trim
03 Cab trim

Cab finished in black suede

The cab headliner and pillars are trimmed in black suede, so the front of the van feels as finished as the back. It ties the cab into the starlight roof and takes away the bare plastic look a panel van usually has.

04 Leisure electrics

What's fitted, and why

Electrics are the part of a van you want to forget about, so we only fit kit from brands we trust. Here's exactly what went into this Caddy.

Renogy

12V 40A DC to DC charger

Charges the leisure battery from the alternator whenever the engine's running, and keeps the starter battery protected.

  • Up to 40A charging, 584W output
  • Works with smart alternators on Euro 6 vans
  • Multi stage charging, set for the battery type
  • Reverse polarity, overvoltage, overcurrent and over temperature protection
Renogy

BT-2 Bluetooth module

Links the charger to the Renogy DC Home app, so charging can be checked from a phone.

  • Live charge readings on your phone
  • Up to around 25m range
  • Draws next to nothing
Renogy

Leisure battery

A deep cycle leisure battery, strapped down beside the charger, runs everything in the back without touching the van's own battery.

240V

Inverter & sockets

An inverter feeds twin UK 240V sockets on the side wall, for laptops, chargers and anything else with a normal plug.

12V

Power panel

A flush panel next to the sockets with everyday car style power.

  • 12V socket, 6A
  • USB charging socket
  • Digital battery voltmeter with on/off
Protection

Fused and tidy

Every circuit is fused, and it's all laid out on one board so it's easy to get at later.

  • Main feed fused in an MTA fuse holder
  • 6 way blade fuse board with blown fuse indicators
  • Negative bus bar for clean earth runs
  • Heavy gauge cable with crimped, heat shrunk lugs
Lighting

Switched lights

The starlight roof and the LED spotlights each have their own switch, so you choose between a soft glow and full working light.
