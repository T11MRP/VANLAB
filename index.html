What we build

From a bare shell to a finished camper

Or one job on its own. Here is the work that comes through the workshop.

Camper conversions

Complete builds. Units, seating, bed, worktop and electrics, spec'd around how you'll actually use the van.

Elevating roofs

Pop-top and elevating roofs with the sleeping deck built in. A standard-height van becomes a four berth without losing the drive.

Beds & seating

Crash-tested rock and roll beds, seat conversions and day-van set-ups, fitted and trimmed to match the interior.

Leisure electrics

DC to DC charging, leisure batteries, fuse boards and 12V or 240V hook-ups, wired in clean and safe.

Windows & heaters

Side and rear-door glass, plus diesel night heaters. Often done same day while you wait.

Lining & starlight

Ply lining, full carpet lining, headliner retrims and fibre-optic starlight roofs for the finishing touch.

About Van Lab

Ten years, one workshop, no shortcuts

Van Lab has been converting vans in Lichfield since 2015, most of them Volkswagens. The approach is simple. We ask how you'll use the van, then build to that.

Honest advice first

If a simpler spec suits you better, we'll say so, and save you money on things you'd never use.

Workmanship you can see

Clean wiring, tidy trims, proper lining. The details are the job, not an afterthought.

Worth the drive

People travel from as far as Scotland for the work.
